## Changes Made

### New Term Created: MONDO:7770736

**Name:** TSEN2-related neurodevelopmental disorder with or without thrombotic microangiopathy

**Definition:** Any neurodevelopmental disorder in which the cause of the disease is a mutation in the TSEN2 gene. This condition is characterized by intellectual disability, growth delay, hypotonia, motor delay, ataxia, vision issues, cardiac features (including left ventricular hypertrophy), and pulmonary complications (such as acute respiratory distress and edema). Brain structural anomalies include pontine and cerebellar hypoplasia, cortical atrophy, and dilated ventricles. Renal features include proteinuria, thrombotic microangiopathy, end-stage kidney disease, and high-severity hypertension.

**Synonyms:**
- "TSEN2-related neurodevelopmental disorder with or without TMA" (EXACT)
- "TRACK syndrome" (RELATED) - acronym for TSEN2 Related Atypical hemolytic uremic syndrome, Craniofacial malformations, Kidney failure
- Full TRACK syndrome expansion (EXACT)
