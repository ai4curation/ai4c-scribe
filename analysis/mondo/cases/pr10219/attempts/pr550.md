# Fix synonym scope on MONDO:0019835 (primary hypophysitis)

Addresses issue #9859.

## Background

@galyea123 reported that MONDO:0019835 (primary hypophysitis) lists "lymphocytic hypophysitis" and "autoimmune hypophysitis" as EXACT synonyms, but this is incorrect:

- **Primary hypophysitis** is a parent category that, per clinical literature, includes several histopathological subtypes:
  - Lymphocytic
  - Granulomatous
  - Xanthomatous
  - IgG4-related
  - Necrotizing
  (plus mixed forms such as lymphogranulomatous / xanthogranulomatous)
- **Lymphocytic hypophysitis** is the *most common* histopathologic subtype, often used interchangeably with **autoimmune hypophysitis** in the literature, but it is not equivalent to the parent.
- Anatomical subclassification (LAH, LINH, LPH) applies specifically to lymphocytic hypophysitis.

References cited by the reporter:
- https://journals.lww.com/neur/fulltext/2018/66020/diagnosis_and_management_of_lymphocytic.23.aspx
- https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK562255/

The reporter noted they are also writing to Orphanet for clarification, so I have deliberately kept changes minimal and reversible.
