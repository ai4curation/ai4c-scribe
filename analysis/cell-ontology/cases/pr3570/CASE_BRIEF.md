## Context

The DN2a (CL_0002423) and DN2b (CL_0002424) thymocyte subtypes are defined based on mouse thymic development staging that does not directly translate to human T cell development. Issue #3500 requested adding taxon constraints to restrict these terms to Mus musculus, preventing their misuse in annotating human datasets where the DN2a/DN2b distinction is not applicable.

## Changes Made

Added 2 new lines to `cl-edit.owl`, one for each term, adding an `in_taxon some NCBITaxon:10090` (Mus musculus) constraint to CL_0002423 (DN2a thymocyte) and CL_0002424 (DN2b thymocyte). This is the standard CL pattern for species-restricted cell types.

## Resolution

Approved on first review in 4 commits. Simple difficulty because adding taxon constraints follows a well-established pattern in CL, and the biological rationale for restricting these terms to mouse is straightforward -- the DN2a/DN2b distinction is based on mouse-specific developmental staging.

# Taxon Constraint Addition to DN2a and DN2b Thymocytes

## Summary
Added explicit "in taxon: Mus musculus" constraints to DN2a (CL_0002423) and DN2b (CL_0002424) thymocyte cell types. This clarifies that the DN2a/DN2b nomenclature and Kit-based staging system (Kit-high vs Kit-low) are specific to mouse thymopoiesis and not reproducibly defined in human thymic development.

## Rationale
The DN1-DN4 staging system for double-negative (DN) thymocytes is well-established in mice but not applicable to human thymus. Specifically:
- DN2a and DN2b are defined by c-Kit (CD117) expression levels exclusively in mouse
- This staging system is not used in human thymocyte development
- Kit-high/Kit-low DN2 subsets are not reproducibly defined in humans
- The existing comments in both terms already note "Observed in mice" but lacked formal taxon annotations

## Changes Made
Modified `src/ontology/cl-edit.owl`:

### For CL_0002423 (DN2a thymocyte)
- **Line 18583**: Added `SubClassOf(obo:CL_0002423 ObjectSomeValuesFrom(obo:RO_0002162 obo:NCBITaxon_10090))`
  - This adds the logical constraint that DN2a thymocytes are restricted to Mus musculus (NCBITaxon:10090)

### For CL_0002424 (DN2b thymocyte)
- **Line 18594**: Added `SubClassOf(obo:CL_0002424 ObjectSomeValuesFrom(obo:RO_0002162 obo:NCBITaxon_10090))`
  - Same taxon constraint for DN2b thymocytes

## Technical Details
- Used OWL relation `RO_0002162` (in_taxon) to formally assert the taxon constraint
- Referenced NCBI Taxonomy ID 10090 for Mus musculus
- Followed the established pattern for taxon constraints used throughout the CL ontology
- Both constraints placed after the logical definitions (EquivalentClasses/inferred SubClassOf) to maintain organizational clarity

## Supporting Evidence
The change is justified by the following publications:
- **PMID:25060579** - Review of mouse DN1-DN4 staging system; DN2a/DN2b defined via Kit expression only in mouse
- **PMID:20543111** - Experimental characterization of DN2a (Kit-high) and DN2b (Kit-low) in adult mouse thymus
- **PMID:32079746** - Human thymus single-cell atlas showing that DN2a/DN2b subsets are not observed or defined in human development
