## Summary

Adds a new class **CL:9900000** (`transitional principal-intercalated cell of kidney collecting duct`) representing the hybrid PC-IC cell type identified in single-cell studies of the human kidney collecting duct. These cells co-express markers of both principal cells (PC) and intercalated cells (IC) and are enriched in chronic kidney disease (CKD).

## Rationale and design decisions

- **Parent term**: The issue requested `CL:1000454 (kidney collecting duct epithelial cell)` as the parent, which sits cleanly above both `CL:1001431` (principal cell) and `CL:1001432` (intercalated cell). Since this cell is described as "transitional/hybrid" between the two, the general kidney collecting duct epithelial cell parent is the most appropriate single is_a. I deliberately did *not* assert is_a both PC and IC — that would over-commit to the equivalence reading of "co-expresses markers."
- **No `EquivalentClasses` axiom**: A logical genus-differentia of `CL:1000454 and part_of some UBERON:0001232` would be vacuous, since `CL:1000454` is already defined that way. Without protein markers (PRO terms) suitable to express the dual PC+IC identity in a reasoner-friendly way, I used only `SubClassOf` axioms, mirroring the pattern used by sibling terms `CL:1001431` and `CL:1001432`.
- **Synonym types**: `tPC-IC cell` is annotated as an `OMO:0003000` abbreviation (related synonym), per issue request and the abbreviation use in PMID:37468583. `hybrid principal-intercalated cell` is a broad synonym since "hybrid" is a more inclusive term than the specific "transitional" relationship described in the definition.
- **ID range**: Used `CL_9900000` from the `Temporary IDs` range (`idrange:81`, `9900000–10000000`) as specified in `CLAUDE.md`. Per the OBO NTR workflow, editors will reassign a final permanent ID at merge time.
