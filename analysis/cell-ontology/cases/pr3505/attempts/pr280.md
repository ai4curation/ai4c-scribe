## Summary

Adds the new term **`fibrochondrocyte progenitor cell`** (FCP) to `src/ontology/cl-edit.owl`. FCP is a mesenchymal progenitor cell in fibrocartilaginous tissue that co-expresses fibrochondrocyte markers (COL1A1, COL3A1) and mesenchymal stem cell markers (MCAM/CD146, MYLK), with colony-forming capacity and multi-lineage (osteogenic/adipogenic) differentiation potential. It is described in single-cell RNA-seq studies of human meniscus (PMID:31871141, PMID:36338137).
