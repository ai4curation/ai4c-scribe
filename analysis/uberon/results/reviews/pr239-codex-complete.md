## Summary

This is curatorially careful but incomplete. It adds four well-modeled
mesosalpinx/antimesosalpinx polarity terms for fallopian tube mucosa and
muscularis, using `adjacent_to` and definitions that explicitly avoid making
the regions parts of the mesosalpinx or antimesosalpinx.

The issue requested eight regional layer terms, including superior and inferior
regions. Those are missing, so the attempt cannot be counted as a full success.
It is nevertheless one of the more thoughtful attempts because it models the
hard polarity distinction correctly and would be a good basis for follow-up.

## Strengths

- Correctly handles mesosalpinx/antimesosalpinx as polarity, not partonomy.
- Uses appropriate mucosa and muscle-layer parents.
- Provides careful definitions and provenance.

## Issues

- Adds only four of the eight requested terms.
- Omits superior and inferior epithelium/muscularis regions.
